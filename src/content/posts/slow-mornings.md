Surabaya wakes up early, and if you want any quiet at all, you have to wake up earlier. By eight the sun is already serious. At six it is still negotiating.

## The routine, such as it is

Water on the stove. Coarse coffee straight into the glass, *tubruk* style, and the patience to let the grounds sink. A notebook open to a fresh page, even if nothing ends up on it.

Some mornings I ride the old bike around the block. It squeaks on every second pedal stroke, and I've decided that's a feature.

## Why it matters

Most of my day belongs to other people: teammates, tickets, the factory systems that can't wait. This hour doesn't. Nobody needs me to ship anything before seven, and that small fact has made me a calmer engineer.

The heat always wins eventually. That's fine. I got there first.
